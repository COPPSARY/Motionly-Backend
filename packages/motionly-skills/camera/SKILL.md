# Camera

Every camera move needs a subject, destination, and settled framing. Use deliberate pushes, pulls, pans, or reframes that reveal meaning. Prefer `power3.inOut` or `power4.inOut` for camera/layout travel. Hold after arrival so the viewer can inspect the result.

Avoid repeated unmotivated zoom/reset cycles. Preserve close prompt framing through a workspace morph unless the story motivates a pullback.
