# Motionly reference-quality bar

The reference product promo is a connected product film, not a sequence of title cards. It uses one visual world, clear scene ownership, editorial scale contrast, real product UI, inline SVG detail, overlapping handoffs, local camera focus, and readable holds. Each scene follows arrival, active transformation, hold, and resolve.

For new compositions, create at least three connected beats when duration permits: establish the product/problem, demonstrate a meaningful transformation, and resolve with a branded outcome or CTA. Use shared colors, shapes, UI surfaces, or typography to bridge scenes. Avoid repeated full-screen fades, generic glass cards, tiny UI, decorative motion without a destination, and simultaneous movement of every layer.

For edits, preserve unaffected scenes and source structure. Make the smallest coherent source change, then check adjacent transitions and final cleanup. Quality never overrides prompt truth, asset fidelity, stable editable IDs, or deterministic seeking.
